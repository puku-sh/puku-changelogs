## What's New

**Welcome to Puku Home** — When you open Puku for the first time, you'll now go through a clean onboarding flow: sign in with Google, accept the privacy policy, and land on a branded home screen with your recent projects and quick actions to get started.

![Puku Home onboarding](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/puku-sh/puku-changelogs/main/changelogs/puku-editor/assets/v1.110.11/puku-home-01.png)

![Puku Home screen with recent projects](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/puku-sh/puku-changelogs/main/changelogs/puku-editor/assets/v1.110.11/puku-home-02.png)

**Clearer Subscription Messages** — If your subscription is inactive or you've hit a usage limit, you'll now see a clear message and a link to your dashboard instead of a confusing error.

## Fixes

- Fixed the editor running out of memory and crashing on 8 GB MacBook Air models
- Fixed a build error that was affecting some development setups
- General stability improvements
