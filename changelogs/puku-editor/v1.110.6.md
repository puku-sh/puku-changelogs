## What's New

**Bigger Context Window** — Puku can now keep track of 300K tokens of context (up from 200K), so it remembers more of your codebase and conversation during long sessions.

**More Reliable Plan Mode** — Plan Mode now tracks each plan correctly, even when you have multiple plans open at once. The "Build" button always targets the right plan.

![Reliable Plan Mode with multiple plans](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/puku-sh/puku-changelogs/main/changelogs/puku-editor/assets/v1.110.6/Reliable-plan.png)

**Plans Can Be Updated** — The agent can now update an existing plan instead of only creating new ones.

**Safer Diagrams in Plans** — If a plan includes a broken diagram, it won't be saved — you'll get a clear error instead of a corrupted plan.

**Fewer Unnecessary Questions** — For simple follow-up requests (like "refine the plan above"), Plan Mode can skip the clarifying questions step.

## Fixes

- Fixed the "Build" spinner getting stuck or showing on the wrong plan when multiple plans were open
- Fixed completed or cancelled plans sometimes reappearing as "in progress"
- Fixed the plan card in chat and the Plan editor getting out of sync
- General stability and accuracy improvements
