## What's New

**New AI Models** — Two new models are now available in the model picker: Puku AI 2.8 (now the default) and Opus 4.8.

**Redesigned Sidebar** — The sidebar now has a cleaner, more compact launcher. You can pin your favorite views, drag and drop to reorder them, and right-click for quick options.

![Redesigned Puku Editor sidebar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/puku-sh/puku-changelogs/main/changelogs/puku-editor/assets/v1.110.8/sidebar-new.png)

**Bigger Context Window** — Puku can now hold 300K tokens of context, so it remembers more during long sessions.

**Smarter Next-Edit Suggestions** — A new suggestion engine predicts your next edit — like updating a renamed variable everywhere it's used, catching typos, or completing a pattern you've started.

**Cleaner File Edit Previews** — Edit previews in chat now show just the relevant changed lines instead of the whole file.

**New Setting: Confirm on Max Requests** — You can now choose whether the agent should ask before continuing past its normal limit on tool calls, instead of always stopping automatically.

**More Private Thinking Output** — The agent's "thinking" steps shown in chat are now cleaned up so internal details never leak into what you see.

## Fixes

- Fixed an issue where a blocked response could get "stuck" and keep blocking every message after it
- General stability and reliability improvements

## Responsible Use of AI Models

> **Notice:** This section is a policy reminder, not a product update.

> Puku Editor is designed to support productive, educational, and professional use cases. To ensure service reliability and compliance with our AI provider policies, requests that involve hacking, cyber abuse, unauthorized system access, malware creation, or other unethical activities may be handled differently or restricted.
>
> Some AI providers maintain strict usage policies and may suspend or limit access if their services are used for harmful or policy-violating purposes. To protect the platform and its users, Puku Editor may route certain requests to alternative models, apply additional safety checks, or decline requests that could facilitate harmful activities.
>
> We encourage all users to use Puku Editor responsibly and in accordance with applicable laws, ethical guidelines, and our Terms of Service.
